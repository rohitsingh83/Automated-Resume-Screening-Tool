How the score works

Five explainable components, weights adjustable in the dashboard.

Component Weight Measured as
Must-have skills 40% required skills found / required
Content similarity (TF-IDF cosine) 25% resume vs job description, rescaled
Experience 15% years / required years
Nice-to-have skills 15% bonus skills found / bonus skills
Education 5% meets degree level

Shortlisted = score ≥ 65 and ≥ 80% must-have skills. Keyword-stuffed resumes lose 15 points and are never auto-shortlisted. Emails and phone numbers are removed before scoring.
